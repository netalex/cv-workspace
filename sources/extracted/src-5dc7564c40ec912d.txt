PROFILE

IT, software and web developer

I have been dealing with various aspects of computer science since almost 15 years, led by a strong personal interest.
I studied in depth the most innovative aspects of software development, especially web/HTML5 and mobile development.
I have been involved in projects based on in-house and opensource CMS platforms, dealing with both operational testing and project design and report, as well as with technical customisations.
Thanks to my web design and communicative skills, I can autonomously manage a web project..
My skills as a software developer allowed me to grasp the conceptual leap from traditional html to HTML5/Ajax/JS as well as the idea of WebApp as a full-fledged software project.
Just to take some examples, I applied the Cordova framework to tackle the convergence and management of different technologies such as java, javascript, hybrid WebApps, and the local management of external services like Google api.

I have been alternating continuous training in the field of web design, which I am interested in on an extracurricular basis too, and traditional training, from a multimedia operator course taken in the very first years of modern web to the most recent tailor-made training on javascript and html5. Moreover, whenever possible I take a “responsive” approach - as regards both technologies and human interaction - even in the most traditional working environments (OracleDB support, PL/SQL development, project analysis, testing, Vertical application and CMS maintenance and customisation…)

I further strengthened my skills in the fields of technical, business and creative writing, so as to be able engage in drafting both non-technical and technical documents, also in english and french, in professional direct and written communication, and in training. With this in mind, I also worked in the field of HR recruitment.

During my professional experience I could face and manage a number of different situations.
I dealt with the working groups organisation and management, with customer relasionships management, and with short but strategic and prompt interventions not only in the fields of analysis, development and system management, but also of hw and sw testing, monitoring and problem solving.

I have always been looking for new opportunities for professional growth and for learning new skills, and hence I have a strong preference for non-repetitive and stimulating tasks, assignments abroad included.
